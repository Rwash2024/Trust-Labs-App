## English (en-US)

**Title**
Trust Labs - معامل ترست

**Short description**
Book lab tests, track your sample and browse Trust Labs branches and packages.

**Full description**
Trust Labs in your pocket. Book your medical tests from your phone, as a home visit or at the branch nearest you, with no calls and no waiting.

What you can do in the app:

• Book tests: choose a branch or a home visit, pick your tests or a package and a date. Our customer service team will contact you to confirm.

• Browse tests and packages: search for any test and see its price, or pick a ready-made checkup package.

• Preparation instructions: find out before you go whether a test needs fasting or any special preparation.

• Our branches: see all branches with their addresses and working hours.

• Sample tracking: check the status of your sample with your phone number.

• Trust Card: your medical file in one place. Your basic details, blood group and emergency file, lab and imaging reports, and your family members. The file opens only with the card code and the registered phone number together.

• Test results: quick access to the results page.

• Rate your visit: tell us how it went in seconds.

• Complaints and suggestions: send your message straight to customer service.

• Lab news and offers.

Your privacy matters: we collect only what is needed to provide the service and we never sell your data. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Hotline: 16183
Email: info@trustlabseg.com

Note: this app is for booking services and viewing information. It does not provide diagnosis or medical advice. Always consult your doctor about your health.
